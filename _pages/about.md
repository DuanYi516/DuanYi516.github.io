# About Me

I am an undergraduate student at [Renmin University of China](https://www.ruc.edu.cn/), majoring in Economics and pursuing a second major in Artificial Intelligence. My research focuses on AI for Science, especially regulatory genomics, multimodal biological foundation models, and reasoning-enhanced modeling for biological sequences.

I have worked on gene expression prediction, regulatory DNA activity prediction, scientific knowledge extraction from life-science literature, and biological foundation model evaluation. I am particularly interested in building models that are not only accurate, but also more biologically grounded and interpretable.

My current research interests include:

- LLMs and foundation models for biological sequences, structures, and language.
- Interpretable modeling for regulatory genomics and gene expression.
- Scientific data construction and evaluation for AI4Science.
